Who we are

Built by the community, for the community.

Project MORE was founded in 2013 by community members in California’s Silicon Valley. We are an all-volunteer organization serving LGBTQIA+ communities in the San Francisco Bay Area, throughout California, and beyond.

We produce cultural events in an affirming and safe environment, connect people with resources, and support fellow nonprofits that share our values.

What we do

Programs that bring people together

  • Qmunity District

    A welcoming, inclusive, and affirming place for LGBTQIA+ people and families on Post Street in downtown San José. It launched in summer 2020 with support from the City of San José.

    Visit the District (opens in a new tab)

  • Health & wellness

    Free community wellness events like the Jubilee festival on Post Street, and pop-up health fairs with testing, vaccinations, and resources. No insurance needed.

  • Arts & culture

    Drag, music, art, and celebrations, from Ice Queens at Downtown Ice to the Qmunity Awards that honor people doing more for the community.

  • MORE Relief Fund

    Emergency financial help for local LGBTQIA+ individuals and families who can’t cover short-term costs like rent or basic needs.

  • Community resources

    A guide to affirming services across Northern California, from mental health and trans care to housing and sexual health.

  • Sports & inclusion

    As a You Can Play ambassador, we work with teams like the San Jose Sharks to make sport welcoming for LGBTQIA+ athletes, staff, and fans.
